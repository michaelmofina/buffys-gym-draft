Not your average gym.

Buffy's doesn't have a traditional open gym floor. Instead, you book a spot in one of our classes with one of our trainers who will guide you through every exercise. Classes are small, so you'll always get the attention you need, and the community is unmatched, so you'll have an awesome crowd cheering you on.

Everyone gets their own book to keep track of their lifts, so you can literally see the progress you're making towards your goals. We welcome all experience levels, and you always get options for exercises that meet you where you're at.

Welcome to our space.

This is where our classes happen. Train alongside a small group, with a coach guiding every lift and plenty of room to find your stride.

Our spacious entrance area is a great place to relax before or after your class. Grab a drink from the fridge and cool down, or just chill out while you make your next move.

Find the right class for you.

Group Strength Training

Mon – Sunday

Each strength training class focuses on a different area, so you can pick something that suits your needs. We'll go through a group warmup, then work on a main lift together, plus some accessory work to keep you well-rounded. You never have to feel pressure to do more than you're able to, and our trainers will personally tailor the exercises to your experience level, and mobility requirements.

1-on-1 & Partner

By appointment

If you prefer an even more focused workout, we offer personal training sessions, or partner training sessions. You'll work with one of our trainers on exactly what you need in our private, quieter training room.

FAQ

Do I need experience to join?

No. We welcome all experience levels. Your coach will guide you through the exercises and adapt them to your experience and mobility.

How many people are in a class?

Classes have a maximum of 10 people, so your coach can give you individual attention.

Can I work out on my own?

We don’t have a traditional open gym floor. Training is coached, through group classes, personal training or partner sessions.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. We offer one-on-one and partner training in our private training room, with sessions tailored to your goals.

Can I try a class first?

Yes — your first class is on us. Use the ‘Book a free class’ link to get started.

Where is the gym?

You’ll find us at 965 College Street in Toronto.
